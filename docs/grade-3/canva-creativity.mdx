# 🎨 Canva & Creativity

*Grade 3 — Digital Explorer*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Templates, text, images and elements**
- **Posters**
- **Invitations**
- **Book covers**
- **Simple visual storytelling**

---

### ⚙️ Templates, text, images and elements

1. Start with a template and click on any text to change what it says.
2. Go to 'Elements' to find shapes, stickers, and icons for your design.
3. Use the 'Uploads' button to add a picture from your computer.

---

### 💡 Posters

**Concept:** Posters are large, eye-catching designs used to share information or celebrate an event.

**Example:** You can create a colorful poster to invite your friends to a science fair.

---

### 💡 Invitations

**Concept:** Invitations are special cards used to ask people to come to a party or gathering.

**Example:** Design a birthday invitation with a fun theme and all the party details.

---

### 💡 Book covers

**Concept:** A book cover shows the title and a picture that hints at what the story is about.

**Example:** Create a cover for your favorite story using a bold title and a cool background image.

---

### 💡 Simple visual storytelling

**Concept:** Visual storytelling uses pictures and a little bit of text to tell a story or explain an idea.

**Example:** Make a series of 3-4 slides in Canva that show the steps of how a seed grows into a flower.
