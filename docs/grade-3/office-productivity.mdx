# 📊 Office Productivity

*Grade 3 — Digital Explorer*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Basic documents; formatting text**
- **Inserting images**
- **Simple tables**
- **Creating a short presentation with text and images**

---

### ⚙️ Basic documents; formatting text

1. Type a short paragraph in Word or Docs.
2. Highlight a word and change its color or font size using the toolbar.
3. Use the 'Alignment' buttons to center your title on the page.

---

### ⚙️ Inserting images

1. Click the 'Insert' menu and select 'Image' or 'Picture.'
2. Choose a picture saved on your computer or search for one online.
3. Resize the image so it fits perfectly with your text.

---

### ⚙️ Simple tables

1. Go to the 'Insert' menu and click 'Table.'
2. Choose how many rows and columns you need (e.g., 3x3).
3. Click inside a cell to start typing information, like a class schedule.

---

### ⚙️ Creating a short presentation with text and images

1. Open PowerPoint or Slides and choose a theme.
2. Add a title slide with your name and the topic.
3. Add a new slide with a few bullet points and a matching picture.
