# 🐱 Scratch & Logic

*Grade 3 — Digital Explorer*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Sprites and backdrops**
- **Sequence**
- **Events**
- **Motion**
- **Dialogue**
- **Simple loops**
- **Interactive stories**

---

### 💡 Sprites and backdrops

**Concept:** Sprites are the characters, and backdrops are the backgrounds or settings for your Scratch project.

**Example:** You can have a fish sprite swimming in an underwater backdrop.

---

### 💡 Sequence

**Concept:** A sequence is the order in which commands are carried out by the computer.

**Example:** If you want a sprite to move then talk, you must put the move block before the say block.

---

### 💡 Events

**Concept:** Events are things that happen that 'trigger' or start a piece of code.

**Example:** The 'When Green Flag Clicked' block is an event that starts your program.

---

### ⚙️ Motion

1. Drag a 'Point in direction' block to change which way your sprite is facing.
2. Use 'Glide' blocks to make your sprite move smoothly across the stage.
3. Experiment with 'Go to random position' to see your sprite jump around.

---

### ⚙️ Dialogue

1. Use the 'Say [Hello!] for 2 seconds' block to make a speech bubble appear.
2. Add another sprite and use the 'Wait' block to make them take turns talking.
3. This creates a simple conversation between characters.

---

### 💡 Simple loops

**Concept:** A loop is a command that tells the computer to repeat a set of instructions many times.

**Example:** Use a 'Repeat 10' block to make a sprite jump up and down ten times.

---

### 💡 Interactive stories

**Concept:** Interactive stories are projects where the viewer can make choices that change what happens.

**Example:** You can program a sprite to ask 'Do you want to go left or right?' and react to the answer.
