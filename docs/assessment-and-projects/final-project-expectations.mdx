# Final Project Expectations

The final project should be age-appropriate and manageable. Students should plan,
create, test, improve and present their work. Individual or small-group projects may
be used depending on the school environment.

| Grade | Expectation |
|---|---|
| [Grade 2](/grade-2/final-project) | Guided digital story, creative poster or Scratch animation. |
| [Grade 3](/grade-3/final-project) | Interactive Scratch story, Canva campaign or digital presentation. |
| [Grade 4](/grade-4/final-project) | Scratch game, Canva campaign or interactive presentation. |
| [Grade 5](/grade-5/final-project) | Scratch game, digital campaign or learning resource. |
| [Grade 6](/grade-6/final-project) | Scratch game, simple website or AI-enhanced digital project. |
| [Grade 7](/grade-7/final-project) | Simple website, Scratch project, digital campaign or AI-assisted creative/learning project. |
