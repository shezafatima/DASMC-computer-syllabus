# 🐱 Scratch & Computational Thinking

*Grade 6 — Digital Builder*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Algorithms; flowcharts**
- **Variables; conditions; loops; operators**
- **Custom blocks**
- **Debugging**
- **Multi-level games and interactive projects**

---

### 💡 Algorithms; flowcharts

**Concept:** A flowchart is a visual diagram that shows the steps of an algorithm using shapes and arrows.

**Example:** Drawing a flowchart for your game logic before you start coding helps you avoid mistakes.

---

### 💡 Variables; conditions; loops; operators

**Concept:** Combining these elements allows you to create complex behavior, such as a sprite that moves faster as the score increases.

**Example:** Use a loop with an operator: 'Repeat until Score > 100.'

---

### ⚙️ Custom blocks

1. Click 'My Blocks' in the toolbox and then 'Make a Block.'
2. Give your block a name like 'Jump' and define the steps (move up, then down).
3. Use your new 'Jump' block whenever you need your sprite to leap, making your code cleaner.

---

### ⚙️ Debugging

1. Use 'Dry Running' — step through your code on paper to see if the logic works.
2. Look for 'Infinite Loops' that might be causing your program to freeze.
3. Use the 'Clean Up Blocks' option to keep your workspace tidy and easy to read.

---

### ⚙️ Multi-level games and interactive projects

1. Use a 'Level' variable to track progress.
2. Switch to a new backdrop whenever the 'Level' changes.
3. Increase the difficulty (e.g., more enemies) as the player moves to higher levels.
