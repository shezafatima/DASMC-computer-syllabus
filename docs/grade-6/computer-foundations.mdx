# 🖥️ Computer Foundations

*Grade 6 — Digital Builder*

Topics covered this grade:

- **System components**
- **File organization**
- **Efficient shortcuts**
- **Troubleshooting**
- **Digital security and responsible device use**

---

### 💡 System components

**Concept:** Understanding how the CPU, RAM, storage, and motherboard work together to make a computer function.

**Example:** The CPU is the 'brain,' RAM is the 'workspace,' and storage is the 'filing cabinet.'

---

### ⚙️ File organization

1. Use tags or colors to mark important or urgent files.
2. Set up an 'In-Progress' and a 'Completed' folder for your big projects.
3. Clean out your 'Downloads' folder once a week to save space.

---

### ⚙️ Efficient shortcuts

1. Use Ctrl + Shift + T to reopen a browser tab you accidentally closed.
2. Use Ctrl + K to quickly insert a hyperlink into your text.
3. Use Windows + V (or Cmd + Ctrl + V) to access your clipboard history.

---

### 💡 Troubleshooting

**Concept:** Troubleshooting is the process of identifying and solving technical problems through logic and testing.

**Example:** If your headphones don't work, check the volume settings, then try them in another device to see if the headphones are broken.

---

### 💡 Digital security and responsible device use

**Concept:** Taking care of your digital health includes using strong passwords, updating software, and limiting screen time.

**Example:** Enable 'Automatic Updates' on your computer so you always have the latest security patches.
