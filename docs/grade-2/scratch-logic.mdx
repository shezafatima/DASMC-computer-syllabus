# 🐱 Scratch & Logic

*Grade 2 — Computer Discovery*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Sequencing activities**
- **Commands and instructions**
- **Scratch interface**
- **Sprites**
- **Simple movement and short animations**

---

### 💡 Sequencing activities

**Concept:** Sequencing means putting steps in the right order so a task gets done correctly.

**Example:** Think of brushing your teeth: first you get the brush, then the paste, then you scrub.

---

### 💡 Commands and instructions

**Concept:** A command is a simple instruction we give to a computer to make it do something.

**Example:** Telling a character in a game to 'Move 10 steps' is a command.

---

### 💡 Scratch interface

**Concept:** The Scratch interface is the playground where we build our games and stories.

**Example:** It has a stage where the characters move and a toolbox full of code blocks.

---

### 💡 Sprites

**Concept:** Sprites are the characters and objects in Scratch that we can program.

**Example:** The orange cat you see when you open Scratch is a sprite.

---

### ⚙️ Simple movement and short animations

1. Drag a 'Move 10 steps' block into the workspace.
2. Click the block to see your sprite move on the stage.
3. Add a 'Turn' block to make your sprite spin around.
