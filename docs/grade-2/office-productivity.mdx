# 📊 Office Productivity

*Grade 2 — Computer Discovery*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Very basic word processing: typing words/sentences**
- **Simple formatting and adding a picture**
- **Introduction to simple presentations**

---

### ⚙️ Very basic word processing: typing words/sentences

1. Open a program like Word or Google Docs.
2. Use the keyboard to type a short sentence about yourself.
3. Press the 'Enter' key to move to a new line.

---

### ⚙️ Simple formatting and adding a picture

1. Select your text and click the 'B' button to make it bold.
2. Click 'Insert' then 'Picture' to add an image from the computer.
3. Drag the corners of the picture to make it smaller or larger.

---

### 💡 Introduction to simple presentations

**Concept:** A presentation is a way to show your ideas using big pictures and a few words on 'slides.'

**Example:** You can make a slide with a picture of your favorite animal and its name.
