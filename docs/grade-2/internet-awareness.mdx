# 🌐 Internet Awareness

*Grade 2 — Computer Discovery*

Topics covered this grade:

- **What is the internet? What is a browser?**
- **Safe browsing with an adult/teacher**
- **Recognizing that not everything online is trustworthy**

---

### 💡 What is the internet? What is a browser?

**Concept:** The internet is a giant library of information that connects computers all over the world. A browser is the app we use to visit that library.

**Example:** Chrome and Edge are browsers that help us 'surf' the internet.

---

### 💡 Safe browsing with an adult/teacher

**Concept:** Safe browsing means only visiting websites that your teacher or parents say are okay.

**Example:** If a screen pops up that you don't recognize, always ask your teacher for help.

---

### 💡 Recognizing that not everything online is trustworthy

**Concept:** Just because something is on a screen doesn't mean it is true. Always check with a grown-up.

**Example:** A video might show something impossible, like a talking dog, which is just for fun and not real.
