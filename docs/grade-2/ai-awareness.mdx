# 🤖 AI Awareness

*Grade 2 — Computer Discovery*

Topics covered this grade:

- **What is AI? Simple examples from everyday life**
- **AI as a computer technology**
- **Basic safety and asking an adult/teacher before using AI tools**

---

### 💡 What is AI? Simple examples from everyday life

**Concept:** AI, or Artificial Intelligence, is a type of computer technology that helps machines 'think' or make decisions.

**Example:** When a tablet recognizes your face to unlock, or a video app suggests a song you might like, that is AI.

---

### 💡 AI as a computer technology

**Concept:** AI is made by people using code to help computers solve problems and learn from information.

**Example:** Just like a student learns from books, an AI learns from data provided by programmers.

---

### 💡 Basic safety and asking an adult/teacher before using AI tools

**Concept:** Always ask your teacher before using any AI tool, just like you ask before going to a new website.

**Example:** If you want to use a tool that draws pictures with AI, check with your teacher first.
