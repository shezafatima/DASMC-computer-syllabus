# 🎨 Canva & Creativity

*Grade 2 — Computer Discovery*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Simple templates**
- **Adding text and images**
- **Basic posters, cards and visual stories**

---

### ⚙️ Simple templates

1. Open Canva and choose a 'Poster' or 'Card' template.
2. Scroll through the choices and click one you like.
3. A template gives you a beautiful starting point for your design.

---

### ⚙️ Adding text and images

1. Click the 'Text' button on the left to add a heading.
2. Click 'Elements' and search for a picture, like 'sun' or 'cat.'
3. Drag the picture onto your design.

---

### 💡 Basic posters, cards and visual stories

**Concept:** You can use Canva to make colorful posters for your room or cards for your friends.

**Example:** Creating a 'Happy Birthday' card with balloons and a picture is a great Canva project.
