# 🖥️ Computer Foundations

*Grade 2 — Computer Discovery*

Topics covered this grade:

- **What is a computer?**
- **Common computer devices**
- **Monitor, keyboard, mouse and basic parts**
- **Turning a computer on/off**
- **Mouse skills; clicking, dragging and selecting**
- **Basic keyboard use**
- **Opening and closing programs**

---

### 💡 What is a computer?

**Concept:** A computer is a smart machine that follows our instructions to help us learn, play, and create.

**Example:** The big machine on your desk with a screen and keyboard is a computer.

---

### 💡 Common computer devices

**Concept:** Computers come in many shapes and sizes, like desktops, laptops, and tablets.

**Example:** A tablet you use for games and a laptop your teacher uses are both computer devices.

---

### 💡 Monitor, keyboard, mouse and basic parts

**Concept:** A computer has different parts that work together: the monitor shows pictures, the keyboard has buttons for typing, and the mouse helps us move things on the screen.

**Example:** Think of the monitor like a TV screen and the keyboard like a board full of buttons.

---

### ⚙️ Turning a computer on/off

1. Locate the power button on the computer case (CPU) or monitor.
2. Press it once gently and wait for the lights to come on.
3. To turn it off, click the 'Start' button on the screen, click 'Power,' and then click 'Shut down.'

---

### ⚙️ Mouse skills; clicking, dragging and selecting

1. Rest your hand gently on the mouse.
2. Click the left button once to select an item.
3. Click and hold the button to 'grab' an item and move it (dragging).

---

### 💡 Basic keyboard use

**Concept:** The keyboard is used to type letters, numbers, and instructions into the computer.

**Example:** You can use the keyboard to type your name in a document or search for a video.

---

### ⚙️ Opening and closing programs

1. Find a picture (icon) on the desktop for the program you want.
2. Double-click it quickly with the left mouse button to open it.
3. To close it, click the small 'X' button in the top-right corner of the window.
