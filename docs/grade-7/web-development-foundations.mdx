# 🕸️ Web Development Foundations

*Grade 7 — Digital Innovator*

Topics covered this grade:

- **How websites work**
- **HTML structure; headings; text; images; links**
- **Basic CSS; colors; typography; spacing**
- **Simple layouts**
- **Building and refining a small website**

---

### 💡 How websites work

**Concept:** Understanding the Client-Server model, DNS, and how browsers render HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

**Example:** When you type a URL, your browser (the client) asks a server for the files, which it then displays.

---

### ⚙️ HTML structure; headings; text; images; links

1. Use 'Semantic HTML' tags like `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, and `<footer>` for better structure.
2. Organize your project with separate folders for 'css' and 'images.'
3. Use 'Relative Paths' (e.g., images/logo.png) so your links work on any computer.

---

### ⚙️ Basic CSS; colors; typography; spacing

1. Use 'External Stylesheets' to keep your CSS separate from your HTML.
2. Master the 'CSS Box Model' — understanding content, padding, border, and margin.
3. Use 'Google Fonts' to add professional typography to your site.

---

### ⚙️ Simple layouts

1. Use 'CSS Flexbox' to easily align items in rows or columns.
2. Apply 'Media Queries' to make your website 'Responsive' (so it looks good on phones too).
3. Use 'Opacity' and 'Transitions' to add subtle hover effects to your buttons.

---

### ⚙️ Building and refining a small website

1. Draft a 'Sitemap' showing how all your pages connect.
2. Build a 'Wireframe' of your homepage before you start coding.
3. Ask friends to test your site and 'Refine' your layout based on their feedback.
