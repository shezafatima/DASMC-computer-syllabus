# 🎨 Canva & Digital Creativity

*Grade 7 — Digital Innovator*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Professional visual hierarchy**
- **Typography and color**
- **Infographics**
- **Presentation storytelling**
- **Campaign and project design**

---

### 💡 Professional visual hierarchy

**Concept:** Using advanced principles like the 'Rule of Thirds' and 'Leading Lines' to create professional-grade compositions.

**Example:** Aligning your most important graphic on one of the grid intersections to make it naturally more appealing.

---

### ⚙️ Typography and color

1. Use 'Color Theory' to pick 'Complementary' or 'Analogous' colors that work together.
2. Adjust the 'Kerning' (space between letters) and 'Leading' (space between lines) for better readability.
3. Pair a 'Display' font for titles with a highly readable 'Body' font for paragraphs.

---

### ⚙️ Infographics

1. Tell a 'Data Story' by choosing the most surprising or important facts to highlight.
2. Use 'Visual Metaphors' (like a growing tree for progress) to explain complex ideas.
3. Ensure your infographic has a clear 'Call to Action' at the end.

---

### 💡 Presentation storytelling

**Concept:** Moving beyond just slides to using narrative techniques to engage and persuade an audience.

**Example:** Starting your presentation with a personal story or a 'Hook' that grabs everyone's attention.

---

### ⚙️ Campaign and project design

1. Create a 'Brand Guide' with your chosen hex codes, font names, and logo variations.
2. Design a consistent 'Multi-Channel' campaign that includes a poster, a website banner, and a presentation.
3. Use Canva's 'Content Planner' to schedule when your designs should be shared.
