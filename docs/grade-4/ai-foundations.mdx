# 🤖 AI Foundations

*Grade 4 — Digital Creator*

Topics covered this grade:

- **What AI is; AI in everyday life**
- **Generative AI; AI assistants**
- **Basic prompting**
- **Brainstorming and learning with AI**
- **AI limitations; privacy and responsible use**

---

### 💡 What AI is; AI in everyday life

**Concept:** AI is a branch of computer science that builds systems capable of doing things that usually require human intelligence.

**Example:** Face recognition on a phone or the way Netflix recommends movies are common AI uses.

---

### 💡 Generative AI; AI assistants

**Concept:** Generative AI is a type of AI that can create new content like text, images, or code. AI assistants help you with tasks and answer questions.

**Example:** ChatGPT and Gemini are AI assistants that can help you brainstorm ideas for a story.

---

### ⚙️ Basic prompting

1. Start with a clear instruction (e.g., 'Write a 3-sentence story about...').
2. Give the AI some context (e.g., '...for a 4th grade class').
3. If the answer isn't quite right, ask the AI to 'try again' with more detail.

---

### ⚙️ Brainstorming and learning with AI

1. Ask the AI for '5 ideas for a science project about plants.'
2. Ask the AI to 'Explain photosynthesis in a simple way for a 10-year-old.'
3. Use the AI's ideas as a starting point for your own original work.

---

### 💡 AI limitations; privacy and responsible use

**Concept:** AI can sometimes make mistakes or share incorrect facts. It is important to protect your privacy and use AI as a tool to help you, not do all your work for you.

**Example:** Never tell an AI your full name, phone number, or where you live.
