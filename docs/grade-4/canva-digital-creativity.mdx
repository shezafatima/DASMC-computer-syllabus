# 🎨 Canva & Digital Creativity

*Grade 4 — Digital Creator*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Design basics; typography; colors; layout**
- **Posters**
- **Presentations**
- **Infographics**
- **Visual storytelling**

---

### 💡 Design basics; typography; colors; layout

**Concept:** Good design uses clear fonts (typography), matching colors, and a balanced layout to share a message.

**Example:** Use a bold font for your title and a lighter one for the details so people know what to read first.

---

### ⚙️ Posters

1. Choose a poster size and a background that isn't too distracting.
2. Add a large, catchy headline that can be read from far away.
3. Include the 'Who, What, Where, and When' for any event you are advertising.

---

### ⚙️ Presentations

1. Use a consistent color scheme across all your slides.
2. Add 'Animate' effects to your text so it appears one point at a time.
3. Include a 'Thank You' or 'Questions' slide at the very end.

---

### 💡 Infographics

**Concept:** An infographic uses pictures, icons, and short text to explain a topic or show data simply.

**Example:** An infographic about recycling could use icons of bottles and cans to show what can be reused.

---

### ⚙️ Visual storytelling

1. Create a series of panels like a comic strip to tell a short story.
2. Use 'Speech Bubbles' and 'Thought Bubbles' for your characters.
3. Choose colors and images that match the mood of your story.
