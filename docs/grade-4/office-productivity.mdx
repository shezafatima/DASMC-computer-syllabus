# 📊 Office Productivity

*Grade 4 — Digital Creator*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Word/Docs: formatting, images and tables**
- **PowerPoint/Slides: structure and visual presentation**
- **Excel/Sheets: simple tables, basic formulas and charts**

---

### ⚙️ Word/Docs: formatting, images and tables

1. Apply 'Styles' to your headings to make your document look professional.
2. Use 'Text Wrapping' so your images sit neatly next to your writing.
3. Add borders and shading to your tables to make them easier to read.

---

### ⚙️ PowerPoint/Slides: structure and visual presentation

1. Plan your presentation with a clear beginning, middle, and end.
2. Use 'Transitions' to make the move between slides look smooth.
3. Keep each slide simple with just a few bullet points and one clear image.

---

### ⚙️ Excel/Sheets: simple tables, basic formulas and charts

1. Enter data into rows and columns to create a clear table.
2. Use the =SUM() formula to add up a list of numbers automatically.
3. Select your data and click 'Insert Chart' to create a colorful bar graph.
