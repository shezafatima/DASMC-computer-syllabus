# 🌐 Internet & Digital Citizenship

*Grade 4 — Digital Creator*

Topics covered this grade:

- **Browsers; effective searching**
- **Evaluating basic information**
- **Passwords; privacy**
- **Phishing/scams**
- **Digital footprint and responsible behavior**

---

### ⚙️ Browsers; effective searching

1. Use specific keywords and quotation marks to find exact phrases.
2. Open multiple tabs to compare information from different websites.
3. Check the 'History' if you need to find a site you visited recently.

---

### 💡 Evaluating basic information

**Concept:** Evaluating information means checking if a website is reliable and if the facts it shares are true.

**Example:** Check if the website has an author or if it is from a well-known organization like a museum or university.

---

### ⚙️ Passwords; privacy

1. Create a 'passphrase' using several random words for a stronger password.
2. Never share your password with anyone, even your best friend.
3. Check the privacy settings on any apps or sites you use with a parent.

---

### 💡 Phishing/scams

**Concept:** Phishing is when someone sends a fake message to try and trick you into giving away your information.

**Example:** If you get an email saying you won a prize but you don't recognize the sender, it might be a scam.

---

### 💡 Digital footprint and responsible behavior

**Concept:** Your digital footprint is the record of everything you do online, which can be seen by others.

**Example:** Always be kind and helpful in your posts, because your digital footprint can last a long time.
